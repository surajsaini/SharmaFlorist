Honor the Women in Your Life with Rose N Petals

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why do we celebrate International Women's Day?

International Women's Day (IWD) is celebrated globally on March 8th. It is a day to recognize women's incredible achievements, raise awareness against bias, and celebrate female empowerment and equality.

When did Women's Day celebrations start in India?

Women's Day has been celebrated in India for decades, gaining immense popularity in recent years as a day to honor the vital roles women play in families, workplaces, and society as a whole.

Why are flowers the best gift for Women's Day?

Flowers symbolize admiration, respect, and appreciation. Sending a fresh bouquet to your mother, wife, sister, friend, or female colleagues is a beautiful, heartfelt way to make them feel special and valued on their dedicated day.

Which areas in Ghaziabad do you deliver Women's Day flowers to?

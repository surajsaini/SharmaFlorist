About Chaudhary Mod Flower Market

Chaudhary Mod Flower Market is the largest and most renowned flower market in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. Located at GT Road (Grand Trunk Road) near Chaudhary Mall, this vibrant market has been serving customers for over 50 years. The market is situated on the historic Grand Trunk Road - one of Asia's oldest roads connecting cities for over 2,500 years.

Address: Chaudhary Mod, GT Road, Near Chaudhary Mall, Ghaziabad - 201001, Uttar Pradesh. Landmarks: Opposite Opulent Mall, near Ghaziabad Railway Station (23 min walk). How to Reach: Shaheed Sthal Metro Station (nearest), Ghaziabad Railway Station, or via GT Road (National Highway).

The market opens at 7 AM and stays open till 11 PM. For the freshest flowers, visit early morning (7-9 AM). Being a wholesale and retail market, you get competitive prices and can compare rates from multiple shops. Parking is available near Chaudhary Mall and on GT Road.

The Jai Mala, also known as Varmala or the wedding garland exchange is one of the most sacred and celebrated rituals of an Indian wedding. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between Jai Mala and Varmala?

Jai Mala and Varmala are the same wedding ritual — the exchange of flower garlands between the bride and groom. "Varmala" is the Sanskrit term while "Jai Mala" is commonly used in North India, especially in Uttar Pradesh weddings in Ghaziabad.

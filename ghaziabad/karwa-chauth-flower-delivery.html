Why do we celebrate Karwa Chauth?

Karwa Chauth is traditionally observed by married Hindu women to pray for their husband's long life and wellbeing. For many families, it is also a time to express love, care and togetherness.

How is Karwa Chauth celebrated?

Customs vary by family. Many prepare with mehndi and festive dress, observe a daytime fast, and gather for evening puja and the Karwa Chauth katha. After sighting the moon, they offer water to it and break the fast. A bouquet and a personal message can accompany your family's celebration.

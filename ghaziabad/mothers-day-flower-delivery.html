Why We Celebrate Mother's Day in India

In India, mothers hold a sacred place in every family. The tradition of honoring mothers has deep roots in Indian culture from ancient scriptures that declare "Matru Devo Bhava" (Mother is equal to God) to the everyday acts of love and sacrifice that Indian mothers are known for.

While Mother's Day is an international celebration that originated in the United States in 1908, Indian families have embraced this occasion wholeheartedly. It gives us a dedicated day to pause our busy lives and truly acknowledge the unconditional love, selfless sacrifices, and tireless devotion our mothers show every single day.

In Ghaziabad and across India, Mother's Day is now celebrated with great enthusiasm children of all ages surprise their mothers with flowers, gifts, sweets, and heartfelt messages. Schools organize special programs, and families come together to make their mothers feel truly valued.

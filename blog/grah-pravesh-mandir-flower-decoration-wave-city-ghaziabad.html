Grah Pravesh Mandir Flower Decoration in Wave City Ghaziabad

August 10, 2026

Fresh Grah Pravesh Decoration for a Home Mandir

Our Sharma Flower Decorators team recently completed a Grah Pravesh mandir flower decoration in Wave City, Ghaziabad. For this Griha Pravesh, also searched as housewarming or home warming decoration, we decorated the family's small indoor home temple with fresh flowers while keeping the sacred space neat, welcoming and suitable for the puja.

Genda Phool and Green Patta Mala Decoration

The design used bright genda phool garlands with green patta mala to create a traditional mandir phool sajawat look. The orange and yellow marigold tones added festive warmth, while the green leaf garlands framed the temple beautifully; customers planning similar puja decor can also see our fresh genda and patta mala service.

Mixed Flower Mala for Bhagwan Murti

We prepared a soft mixed flower garland, or phool mala, for the Bhagwan murti inside the mandir. The mala was sized for the small idol so it looked graceful without covering important details, and every flower was arranged carefully for a fresh, balanced and devotional finish during the Grah Pravesh puja.

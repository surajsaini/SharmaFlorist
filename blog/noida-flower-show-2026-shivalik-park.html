Spring is Here! Don’t Miss the Noida Flower Show at Shivalik Park (Feb 19-22)

February 19, 2026

Are you looking for a colorful and fun day out with your family in Noida? The season of spring is arriving, and that means it is time for the annual Noida Flower Show!

This is one of the most awaited events in the city for nature lovers, gardeners, and photographers.

Who is Organizing It and Why?

This beautiful event is organized together by the Floriculture Society and the Noida Authority. They organize this show every year to celebrate the arrival of spring. The goal is to encourage people to love nature, learn about gardening, and make our city greener.

When is It?

Mark your calendars for four days of floral fun: Dates: February 19 to February 22 (Thursday to Sunday).

Where is It and How to Reach?

The show is happening at Shivalik Park, located near Sector 33/34 in Noida.

How to reach: It is easily accessible by road. If you are using the Delhi Metro, the nearest stations are usually Noida City Centre or Wave City Centre. From there, you can take a quick auto-rickshaw ride to the park.

What Flowers Can You See?

Get your cameras ready! You will see thousands of beautiful flowers in full bloom. While the exact variety changes every year, you can typically expect to see:

  • Huge and colorful Dahlias.
  • Many varieties of Roses.
  • Bright Petunias, Marigolds, and Pansies.
  • Amazing Bonsai plants (miniature trees).
  • Creative floral displays and garden designs made by competitors.

What Else Can Visitors Do and Eat?

It’s not just about looking at flowers. It’s a complete fair!

  • Gardening Shopping: There are usually many stalls selling seeds, pots, gardening tools, fertilizers, and baby plants that you can buy for your home garden.
  • Food Stalls: You will find plenty of food stalls selling delicious local snacks, street food, ice creams, and drinks. It’s a great place for a picnic lunch.
  • Photography: It is the perfect spot for Instagram photos and family portraits among the blooms.

Tickets and Parking

Tickets: Entry to the Noida Flower Show has historically been Free for everyone. This makes it a budget-friendly outing.

Parking: Yes, there is generally parking available near Shivalik Park. However, since it is a very popular event, it can get crowded on weekends. It is better to reach early or use public transport if possible.

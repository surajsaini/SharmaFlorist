A Colorful Weekend Ahead: The Greater Noida Annual Flower Show Starts Feb 26

February 26, 2026

If you missed the Noida flower show, or just can't get enough of beautiful blooms, don't worry! Greater Noida is ready to host its own spectacular spring event a week later. The Greater Noida Authority has announced its annual flower show, promising a visual treat for visitors.

Who is Organizing It and Why?

This event is organized directly by the Greater Noida Authority. They host this event to showcase beautiful landscaping, promote environmental awareness among residents, and provide a cheerful, colorful gathering space for the community during the best weather of the year.

When is It?

This is also a four-day event, perfect for a weekend visit: Dates: February 26 to March 1.

Where is It and How to Reach?

The specific park venue in Greater Noida is usually announced closer to the date (often held at major locations like City Park in Alpha 1, but please check local listings for the confirmed venue).

How to reach: Greater Noida is well connected by wide roads. You can easily drive there. If using public transport, you can use the Aqua Line Metro towards Greater Noida and take local transport to the venue.

What Flowers Can You See?

Greater Noida is known for its wide-open green spaces, and the flower show reflects that. Visitors can usually see:

  • Large displays of seasonal winter flowers like Antirrhinum (Snapdragons), Salvia, and Dianthus.
  • Impressive collections of potted plants and hanging baskets.
  • Often, they feature unique attractions like vegetable carving displays or cactus corners.

What Else Can Visitors Do and Eat?

It is a perfect setting for a relaxed family outing.

  • Enjoy the Vibe: The venues in Greater Noida are often spacious, allowing kids plenty of room to walk around.
  • Food and Drinks: Like most fairs, there will be vendor stalls offering tasty snacks, tea, coffee, and soft drinks to keep you energized while you explore.
  • Learn: Sometimes there are small workshops or information desks about home composting or taking care of plants.

Tickets and Parking

Tickets: Entry to this flower show is also typically Free for the public.

Parking: Ample parking space is usually provided by the Authority near the event venue for visitors arriving by car.
